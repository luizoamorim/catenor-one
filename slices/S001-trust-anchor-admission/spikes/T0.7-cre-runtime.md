## HTTP / runtime limits

Simulator defaults (`cre workflow limits export`) are **not** production values. `--limits default` also applied the simulator defaults (6 calls allowed), so limits were tested with a production-like file (`limits.production-like.json`: `HTTPAction.CallLimit 5`, `RequestSizeLimit 10kb`, `ResponseSizeLimit 100kb`, `Consensus.CallLimit 20`, values from docs.chain.link/cre/service-quotas). Results are **simulator enforcement with a production-like limits file**, not production behavior.

| Limit | Documented production | Simulator default | Observed with production-like file |
|---|---|---|---|
| `HTTPAction.CallLimit` | 5 | 15 | calls 1–5 OK; call 6 → `[101]LimitExceeded … cannot use 6, limit is 5` |
| `HTTPAction.RequestSizeLimit` | 10 KB | 120 KB | 9,216 B OK; 11,264 B → `[8]ResourceExhausted … exceeds the simulation limit of 10000 bytes`. **"kb" = 1,000 bytes**; the limit applies to the raw (decoded) body, not its base64 form |
| `HTTPAction.ResponseSizeLimit` | 100 KB | 250 KB | 99,000 B OK; 101,000 B → `[8]ResourceExhausted … limit of 100000 bytes` |
| `HTTPAction.ConnectionTimeout` | 10 s (connection establishment) | 10 s | — |
| per-request `timeout` (`google.protobuf.Duration`, e.g. `"8s"`) | docs: default 5 s, "default maximum" 10 s | — | `2s` + 3 s delay → `context deadline exceeded`; `8s` + 7 s delay → OK; `15s` **accepted** (3 s delay — whether values > 10 s take effect is **UNCONFIRMED**); no field + 7 s delay → OK (the simulator's default is ≥ 7 s, not the documented 5 s) |
