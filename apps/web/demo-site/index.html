The thesis

Entitled isn’t eligible.

  1. 0of 4 HBAR

The stack

Four technologies.
One job each.

Catenor

Identity and scoped authority.

Every actor has its own did:catenor; a wallet is only an account bound to it. Every action needs explicit, scoped authority, and delegation never exceeds the delegator.

Chainlink CRE

Confidential, current eligibility.

A deployed Confidential Workflow reads today’s identity evidence inside a TEE and decides. Only facts, decisions and commitments leave the enclave.

Privy

Which key can sign what.
  • Signature wallets: messages only, never money.
  • SPV wallet: the ATS Factory and its own equity.
  • Agent wallet: only Lisa or Bart, ≤ 20 HBAR.
  • Investor wallets: receive-only.
